## 本次更新

首版发布后的第一轮打磨，集中在「真的在厨房里用」时最容易卡住的三个点。

### 新增

- **加一分钟**：运行中的步骤旁新增 `+1'` 按钮。火候差一点、汤还要收一会儿，不必停表重开
- **通知权限申请**：Android 13 及以上会主动申请通知权限，否则到期提醒发不出来
- **前台计时通知**：有计时在跑时，通知栏常驻显示「哪一步、还剩多久」

### 改进

- 到点只提醒、不再改动步骤状态：此前提醒会把状态打回 pending，「+1 分钟」无处可点
- 步骤行的操作区重排：暂停/继续、完成、+1 分钟的触控区域各自独立，不再误触

## What's new

The first round of polish after launch, focused on the three places that actually bite while cooking.

### Added

- **Add a minute**: a `+1'` button next to any running step. The heat was not quite right or the sauce needs longer — no need to stop and restart the timer
- **Notification permission request**: on Android 13 and above the app now asks for notification permission, without which due alerts cannot be delivered
- **Foreground timer notification**: while timers run, the notification shows which step and how long is left

### Improvements

- Alerts no longer mutate step state: a due alert used to push the step back to pending, which left "+1 minute" with nothing to act on
- Reworked the action area of a step row: pause/resume, done and +1 now have separate touch targets
