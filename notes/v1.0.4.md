## 本次更新

两件事：**到点提醒现在会一直响到你点「知道了」**，以及修掉了「加一分钟」加不满一分钟的毛病。

### 新增

- **持续到点提醒**：以前每一步到点只响一两秒，人不在手机边上就完全错过。现在会持续响铃 + 震动，直到你点通知上的「知道了」，或回到「在煮」页点「+1 分钟」续上
- **提醒方式可选**：设置页新增「到点提醒」，三档任选——响铃 + 震动（默认）、仅震动、仅通知。旁边有「试听」，点一下就能确认效果

### 修复

- **「加一分钟」只加了二十秒**：到点后计时会停在 00:00，但内部到期时间会越走越远。原来是在这个已经过去的时刻上加一分钟，加的时间被「已经过去多久」吃掉了——归零 40 秒后点只加 20 秒，归零 60 秒后点等于没反应。现在以「当前时刻」为准，任何时候点都是实打实的一分钟
- **「+1」按钮太不显眼**：原来只是个没有边框的小字，现在是有描边的按钮，文案改为「+1 分钟 / +1 min」
- **到点后看不出已经过了多久**：归零的步骤现在额外标出「已到点 / Overdue」，不再和刚跳到 00:00 的瞬间长得一样

## What's new

Two things: **timer alarms now ring until you tap "Got it"**, and "add one minute" actually adds a full minute again.

### Added

- **Persistent alarm**: previously a finished step only made a one-second sound, which is easy to miss when you are not next to the phone. It now rings and vibrates continuously until you tap "Got it" on the notification, or tap "+1 min" on the cooking screen to continue
- **Selectable alarm style**: a new "Timer alarm" section in Settings offers sound + vibrate (default), vibrate only, or notification only. A "Try it" button lets you confirm what it will do

### Fixed

- **"Add one minute" only added twenty seconds**: once a step reaches zero the timer stays at 00:00 while its internal deadline keeps sliding further into the past. The old code added a minute to that stale deadline, so the already-elapsed time ate most of it — tapping 40 seconds after zero added only 20 seconds, and tapping 60 seconds after zero did nothing visible. It now counts from the current moment, so a minute is a minute whenever you tap
- **The "+1" control was too easy to miss**: it was bare text with no border. It is now an outlined button reading "+1 min"
- **No way to tell how far past due a step was**: finished steps now also show "Overdue", instead of looking identical to the instant they hit 00:00

**A note on alarm settings**: if you previously turned off sound or vibration for StoveSide at the system level, Android remembers that. Use "Try it" in Settings to confirm, or allow "Timer finished (alarm)" in your system notification settings.
